# Definition

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}p(t)}{\mathrm{d}t} = -\int^{t} K(t-t')  \frac{p(t')}{m} \, \mathrm{d}t' + \zeta(t)
$$

^7e2757

where $K(t)$ is the [[Memory kernel|memory kernel]] and $\zeta(t)$ is a random force that satisfies the fluctuation-dissipation theorem
$$
\langle \zeta(t) \zeta(t') \rangle = \frac{1}{\beta} \, K(t-t')
$$
